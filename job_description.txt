AI Solutions Engineer

As an AI Solutions Engineer, you'll have the opportunity to understand business needs and challenges, design and build intelligent AI agents, and lead AI-powered solutions from requirements gathering through full implementation in a production environment.

What You'll Do
- Work directly with customers to map processes, understand business needs, and translate them into technological solutions.
- Design architectures and build intelligent AI agents.
- Develop integrations with APIs, databases, and enterprise systems.
- Test, optimize, and improve solution performance.
- Lead end-to-end implementation processes, ensuring successful deployment to production.

What We're Looking For
- A bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering and Management / Software Engineering / Computer Science / another relevant technology-related field.
- Up to one year of professional experience in project management, software development, or analyst roles.
- Knowledge of and experience with AI technologies.
- Strong systems thinking, analytical skills, and the ability to solve complex problems.
- Excellent interpersonal skills, business acumen, and the ability to communicate and work directly with customers.
- A proactive and independent mindset, with the ability to take solutions from concept to implementation.
